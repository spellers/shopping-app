How it works
  1. Match ingredients to Tesco products from the meal pages or persistent ingredients (the "Find in Tesco" button uses the top catalogue match).
  2. Generate a shopping list from a completed vote - matched items show the Tesco product and price.
  3. On the shopping list, press "Add to Tesco basket" to push every matched item into your basket in one go.
  4. Sign-in lasts ~30 days (session stored in ~/.basketeer/session.json). Checkout always finishes in a browser - you complete payment yourself.

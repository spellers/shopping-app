Getting started
  1. Your meal library — the Meal Tracker is a library of every meal you’ve entered, shown as tiles. Click a tile to open it and add or remove ingredients, then use meals as options in votes or on your shopping list. New installs come with a few simple starter meals — edit them to make the app your own.
  2. Pantry staples — things you keep stocked (rice, oil, soy sauce) go in Persistent Ingredients rather than on each meal. Mark them Shared if one unit covers every meal, or Per meal if each meal needs its own.
  3. Vote on dinner — create a Vote with a few meal options, share the page, and everyone picks up to three favourites. The results screen shows who’s voting and the current tally.
  4. Build your shopping list — from the vote results, select the winning meals and hit Create shopping list. Ingredients are combined automatically — shared goods appear once, per-meal goods are multiplied by the number of meals that use them.
  5. Buy and tick off — on the Shopping List page, tick items as you shop. Matched Tesco products show their price, and you can send the whole list to your Tesco basket in one click.
A few things worth knowing
  • The Tesco integration needs you to sign in once with your Tesco account (a browser window opens). If the connection drops after a system restart, just sign in again from the Tesco page.
  • Updating: the app checks for new versions in the background. When one is available, a banner appears at the top of the page — download it and run the installer; your data is kept.
  • Your data (meals, votes, shopping list) is stored locally on this computer, so everything works offline except Tesco product searches.

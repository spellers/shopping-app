Getting started
  1. Your meal library — the Meals page is a library of every meal you’ve entered, shown as tiles. Click a tile to open it and add or remove ingredients, then use meals as options in votes or on your shopping list. New installs come with a few simple starter meals — edit them to make the app your own.
  2. Ingredients — the Ingredients page is a list of all ingredients that can be assigned to your meals.
  3. Import a recipe experimental — on the Meals page, paste a recipe link (for example, this BBC Good Food recipe) into the import box next to New Meal. The app creates the meal and adds its ingredients — any ingredients you don’t have yet are added to your Ingredients list automatically. It works on most recipe websites, but not all sites can be read.
  4. Vote on dinner — create a Vote with a few meal options, share the page, and everyone picks up to three favourites. The results screen shows who’s voting and the current tally.
  5. Build your shopping list — from the vote results, select the winning meals and hit Create shopping list. Ingredients are combined automatically — shared goods appear once, per-meal goods are multiplied by the number of meals that use them.
  6. Buy and tick off — on the Shopping List page, tick items as you shop. Matched Tesco products show their price, and you can send the whole list to your Tesco basket in one click.
A few things worth knowing
  • The Tesco integration needs you to sign in once with your Tesco account (a browser window opens). If the connection drops after a system restart, just sign in again from the Tesco page.
  • Updating: the app checks for new versions in the background. When one is available, a banner appears at the top of the page — click Update now and the app updates and restarts itself; your data is kept.
  • Your data (meals, votes, shopping list) is stored locally on this computer, so everything works offline except Tesco product searches.

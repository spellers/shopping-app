How it works
  1. Match ingredients to products from the meal pages or persistent ingredients — "Find in {{ grocer.name }}" always searches the supermarket you have selected.
  2. Generate a shopping list from a completed vote — matched items show the product and price.
  3. On the shopping list, press "Add to {{ grocer.name }} basket" to push every matched item in one go.
  4. Checkout always finishes on the supermarket’s own site — you complete slots and payment yourself.
